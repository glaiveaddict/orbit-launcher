# Changelog

## v2.13.0
- **Idle screen:** if you don't touch the mouse or keyboard for 30 seconds, Orbit's menu fades away and only your background shows. Move the mouse and everything comes back. It waits while a window is open or you're typing, and you can turn it off on the Appearance page.

## v2.12.0
- **Speedrun timer (Premium):** an on-screen timer over Roblox with splits and a personal best for every game. Ctrl+Alt+T starts and stops it, Ctrl+Alt+Y splits, Ctrl+Alt+U resets (Boost page).
- **Join when space opens (Premium):** pick a full server and Orbit keeps checking it, then joins you the moment a spot frees up. Works from a join link, a server in the list, or a player's card when Roblox shows which server they're in.
- **Shrink for Discord (Premium):** one click turns a clip into a copy under 10 MB so it fits in a Discord message.
- **10 new Premium themes:** Galaxy, Firefly Night, Ruby, Ocean Deep, Vaporwave, Toxic, Candy, Midnight Rose, Arctic and Sunset Drive.
- **New Premium backgrounds:** Fireflies and Holo.
- **New Premium sound packs:** Arcade and Crystal.
- **Premium crosshairs and cursors:** Gold Halo, Diamond Sight, Plasma Ring, Royal Cross, Gold Arrow, Holo Ring and Crown.
- A welcome screen when you activate Premium, and a shiny Premium badge.

## v2.11.0
- **Custom skies fixed:** installing a sky now turns off Plain gray sky, which was replacing it with a flat light sky (it was on for BedWars). There's also a new **Hide Roblox's clouds** switch in the Pack store, so custom skies aren't covered by clouds. Orbit also warns you when your texture quality is on Lowest, which makes skies blurry.
- **Discover:** a new page with what's trending on Roblox and a game search. Play in one click or add games to your Library.
- **Orbit Wrapped (Premium):** your month, your year or all time as a story, plus a shareable image (Stats page).
- **Auto-tune (Premium):** Orbit checks your graphics card, CPU, memory and screen and picks the best settings (Boost page).
- **Auto-setups (Premium):** link a saved setup to a game in your Library, and it switches on when you launch that game.
- **Auto-clip lag spikes (Premium):** saves an instant replay when your ping suddenly jumps.
- **Theme maker (Premium):** make your own Orbit theme and share it with a code (Appearance page).
- **AFK kick warning:** a heads-up about 3 minutes before Roblox kicks you for being idle.
- **Private servers:** save private server links for each game and join in one click (the ⋯ button on Library games).
- **Daily time limit per game:** a heads-up 10 minutes before, and when you hit it.
- **Share codes:** share your crosshairs, cursors, setups and themes as a code a friend can import.
- **Weekly recap to Discord:** posts your recap every Sunday. Friend and game update alerts can go to Discord too.

## v1.9.1
- **Fix:** the server badge and live tracking could stop working with FPS Boost on. With Roblox on high priority and an unlimited frame rate, Windows' own tools slowed down so much that Orbit mistook its tracker for a dead one and replaced it, and the badge never got drawn. Orbit now checks processes directly (instantly, no slow Windows tools) and keeps its own badge and tracker responsive.
- **Gentler priority:** Roblox now gets above-normal priority instead of high, so your PC, Discord and overlays stay smooth.
- A warning on the Boost page when the frame rate cap is set to Unlimited.
