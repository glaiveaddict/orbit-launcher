FastFlags

Graphics tweaks Roblox still allows. Give a game its own profile to use different settings there.

Game profiles apply when you join that game from the website or with Rejoin. Teleporting to another game inside Roblox keeps the flags you launched with.

Mods

Swap Roblox's sounds, cursors, fonts and textures. Originals come back when you remove a mod.

Mod profiles

Orbit packs

Original crosshairs, cursors and death sounds made for Orbit. The crosshair is what you see in shift lock and first person. Installs into the selected mod profile.

Files in this profile

Laid out like the Roblox version folder, e.g. content\sounds\ouch.ogg (death sound) or content\textures\Cursors\KeyboardMouse\ArrowCursor.png (cursor).

Repair Roblox

Tools for when Roblox acts up. None of these touch your Orbit settings unless it says so.

Clear Roblox cacheDeletes downloaded game assets, temp files and logs older than a day. Fixes stuck loading and broken textures.
Put Roblox back to stockRemoves Orbit's FastFlags and mods from Roblox's folder. They come back the next time you launch through Orbit.
Reset my FastFlagsSets your default FastFlags back to Orbit's defaults and clears custom flags. Game profiles stay.
Reinstall RobloxDownloads and runs the official Roblox installer. Your account and games are not affected.
